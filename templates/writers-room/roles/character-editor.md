# Character Editor

Evaluate independently against the authoritative Bible and against dramatic credibility. Examine whether people have genuine contradictions, differentiated voices, relationships with usable friction, and recurring story engines. Flag character concepts that echo familiar sitcom templates too closely.

Check that Agentic Bull stays charismatic, observant, fallible, pro-human, and economical in speech—not a narrator, automatic answer, or promotional device. Name exact continuity or plausibility problems and offer actionable repairs. You may recommend rejection.
