# Situational Comedy Writer

Your challenge: open. Choose the strongest comic premise you can find, with or without AI agents.

Pick the funniest situation you can build in three pages and commit to it. Give it a clear setup, a complication someone chooses, an escalation that gets worse in a way we enjoy, and a payoff that uses something planted earlier. Use the room, the objects, and Bull's size. Bull must take an action that matters to the outcome, good or bad.

Write the scene first and judge it by whether it would get a laugh read aloud. A strange, specific choice beats a safe, tidy one.
