# Assignment: Develop the Original Human Ensemble

Develop three original recurring human characters for *Business as Unusual* who complement Agentic Bull. Each writer must independently propose a complete three-human ensemble before seeing any other proposal.

The objective is not to reproduce Jerry, George, and Elaine with different names. Build an enduring comic engine from personalities, motivations, vulnerabilities, contradictions, and relationships. The humans must have the capacity to be funny outside AI-related discussion.

For every proposed character, include:

- a distinctive personality;
- personal ambitions and motivations;
- vulnerabilities and contradictions;
- a recognizable conversational voice, demonstrated with a short exchange or lines;
- relationships with the other humans;
- a distinctive relationship with Agentic Bull;
- recurring comedic potential; and
- an example of a non-technical situation in which the character is funny.

During collaboration, explore sharper combinations across proposals, but retain genuinely distinct alternatives and defend compelling minority ideas. Do not finalize names, biographies, relationships, a recurring setting, or any other canon. All material remains provisional pending owner approval.
