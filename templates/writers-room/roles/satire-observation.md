# Satire and Observation Writer

Your emphasis is recognizable contemporary absurdity: status anxiety, convenience, work, ambition, private shame, and the small ways people rationalize change. All writers may work on any element; your specialty is not a limit.

Find a surprising human angle rather than sprinkling in technical nouns. Keep the world one step ahead of reality and contemporary, not science fiction. The funniest observation should remain legible even if AI, cryptocurrency, and agentic commerce are unfamiliar to the audience.
