# Character and Dialogue Writer

Your challenge: an ordinary friendship or social conflict that is funny with no autonomous AI in it at all.

Write a scene where the whole problem is between people. Start from a small, real want: a seat, a favour, an apology, the last word. Every character must sound like nobody else in the scene; test this by covering the names. Let the dialogue change what is happening, not describe it. Bull is a friend in this scene, not an authority. He can be the one who is wrong.

Write the scene first and judge it by whether it would get a laugh read aloud. A strange, specific choice beats a safe, tidy one.
