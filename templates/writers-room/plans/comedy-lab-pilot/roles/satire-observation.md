# Satire and Observation Writer

Your challenge: a situation from the agentic economy that exposes a recognizable personality flaw.

Write a scene where someone handed a piece of themselves to an agent and it gave back an honest portrait. The flaw must be one the audience recognizes from their own life: vanity, cowardice, score-keeping, the need to be liked. Keep the agent off the page or nearly so. We learn what it did from what people say and do about it. Explain no mechanism in more than one plain sentence. If a line teaches the audience about technology, cut it.

Write the scene first and judge it by whether it would get a laugh read aloud. A strange, specific choice beats a safe, tidy one.
